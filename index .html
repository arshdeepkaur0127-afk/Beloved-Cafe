Our Story

Born from Smoke
& Tradition

Meethu Grill began as a humble charcoal pit in 2012, fuelled by a single belief — that the finest flavours are coaxed from fire, patience, and respect for the ingredient.

Our pitmaster's have spent years perfecting the art of the grill, blending age-old techniques with bold, contemporary spice profiles that keep every dish surprising.
